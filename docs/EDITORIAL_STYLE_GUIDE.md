# Kreatazz Editorial Style Guide

This guide keeps website and blog content human, useful, and consistent.

## Goal

Write like an experienced operator sharing practical lessons, not like a generic marketing engine.

## Voice and Tone

- Write in plain business language.
- Use short, direct sentences where possible.
- Prefer concrete examples over abstract claims.
- Sound confident, but not exaggerated.
- Avoid hype words unless they add real meaning.

## Default Structure for Blog Posts

Use one of these patterns and rotate between them.

### Pattern A: Problem -> Reality -> Approach -> Outcome

- Open with a real operational problem.
- Explain why common approaches fail.
- Describe a practical approach.
- Close with measurable outcomes or decision guidance.

### Pattern B: Field Note -> Lessons -> Next Step

- Start with a short real-world observation.
- Share 2-4 lessons from execution.
- End with a practical next step for leaders.

### Pattern C: Myth -> What Actually Works -> Checklist

- State a common misconception.
- Replace it with a practical alternative.
- End with a simple checklist readers can apply.

## Humanization Rules

- Use everyday words before jargon.
- Replace broad claims with specifics.
- Mention tradeoffs and constraints honestly.
- Do not use fake urgency.
- Avoid repetitive phrase templates across posts.

## Avoid These Patterns

- "In today's rapidly evolving landscape..."
- "Unlocking transformative synergies..."
- "Revolutionize your business with..."
- Paragraphs that repeat the same point with different buzzwords.

## Author Policy

- Do not use personal bylines unless the article is genuinely written by that person.
- Use team-level ownership only when accurate.
- If uncertain, omit author field.

## SEO Without Robotic Writing

- Include one clear primary keyword naturally in title and intro.
- Use related terms where context fits.
- Keep excerpts specific and readable.
- Prioritize reader clarity over keyword density.

## Readability Targets

- Title: 8-14 words.
- Excerpt: 14-26 words.
- Paragraph length: 2-5 lines on desktop.
- Reading time: usually 5-9 minutes for core posts.

## Quality Checklist Before Publish

- Is the opening specific and relevant?
- Can a leader act on this content?
- Does the post avoid repeated buzzword phrases?
- Are outcomes framed realistically?
- Does it sound like a real team wrote it?

## Internal Consistency

- Keep terminology aligned with site positioning: operational intelligence, workflow intelligence, execution quality, decision support.
- Do not overuse any single phrase in every paragraph.
- Use sentence variety to avoid AI-like rhythm.

## Content Integrity

- No fabricated customer names, stats, or case studies.
- If numbers are estimated, label them clearly.
- If examples are hypothetical, say so.
